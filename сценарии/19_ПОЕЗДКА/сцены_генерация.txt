PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 48-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«Mamá, tu habitación la usará la madre de Iñaki», dijo mi hija. El viaje lo había pagado yo". Story: a 67-year-old widow, a retired pharmacy assistant in a northern Spanish port city, pays for a family trip to the Canary Islands for her daughter's twentieth wedding anniversary. Her daughter tells her not to come: her room will go to the son-in-law's mother. The booking is in the widow's name, so only she can give up her place; she cancels her own room and keeps the rest as a gift. The co-mother-in-law turns ally and reveals the son-in-law has resented her for twenty years because of a cruel remark she made in a restaurant bathroom at the wedding. She apologises to him at the harbour pilots' pier where her husband died; he confesses her daughter had asked for a divorce. She asks him to cancel his staged vow renewal and simply listen. The two widowed co-mothers-in-law sail up the coast of Norway in December, and she opens her late husband's brass compass at the North Cape.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, soft grey Atlantic light in the city then deep blue Arctic winter light, warm interiors, shallow depth of field, palette of slate, teal, rust red and brass, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.
