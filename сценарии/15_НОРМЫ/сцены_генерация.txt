PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 55-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«Norma número uno: llamar antes de entrar», pegó mi nuera en la nevera. Yo puse otra hoja al lado". Story: a 66-year-old widow, a retired operating-room nurse, moves in with her son's family while her flooded flat is repaired. On the ninth day her daughter-in-law sticks a handwritten list of house rules on the fridge. That night the widow pins beside it the family loan contract for the house's down payment, with eight unpaid boxes. The daughter-in-law thought the son was paying. The widow follows her son and discovers he lost his job in March and has spent eight months sitting on a tiled bench in a big city park in his suit; he has emptied the grandchildren's savings and pawned his late father's silver pocket watch. She redeems the watch, rewrites the loan on fair terms, finds him a job in a railway workshop, and the two women become allies. Autumn to spring in an Andalusian city, its riverside old quarter and a suburb of terraced houses.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, soft golden Andalusian autumn light, warm interiors, shallow depth of field, palette of whitewash, terracotta, orange-tree green and navy, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.
