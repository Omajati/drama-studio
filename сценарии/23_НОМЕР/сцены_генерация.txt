PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 49-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«Ya no puedes vivir sola, mamá», dijo mi hija. Desde el hospital marqué mal un número". Story: a 72-year-old widow, a retired building concierge, lies in hospital after hip surgery while her daughter announces she will take her to Madrid. Dialling her son from memory she gets one digit wrong and reaches the woman she secretly sheltered as a pregnant sixteen-year-old in 1994 in the concierge lodge storeroom. An hour later that woman walks in: she is a nurse supervisor in the same hospital. The hospital chart says the widow's house is no longer available. The nurse finds tourists in the house, the late husband's uniform in rubbish bags on the roof, and online reviews showing the son-in-law has rented the house to tourists every time she travelled for two years. The widow sets conditions, refuses to leave, and is carried home up the steep lane by her husband's old fellow guards. The physiotherapist who helps her walk again is the baby born in 1994, named after her.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, warm Andalusian light, red-gold sunsets, cool hospital whites, shallow depth of field, palette of whitewash, terracotta, jasmine green and baby-jacket yellow, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.

Recurring characters (keep identical across shots):
MILA — a 72-year-old Southern European woman, short wavy grey hair, round face, small pearl earrings; in hospital a pale blue gown; at home a lilac cardigan. At 40 in 1994: dark hair, concierge's apron.
ROCÍO — the 48-year-old nurse supervisor, dark hair in a bun, white nursing uniform with a lanyard badge; off duty jeans and a grey jumper. At 16 in 1994: dark braids.
ESTHER — the 45-year-old daughter, straight brown hair, tired face, camel coat.
CARLOS — the 50-year-old son-in-law, gelled hair, expensive watch, navy blazer.
MILAGROS — the 32-year-old physiotherapist, very short dark hair, navy polo shirt, sports shoes.
RAFAEL — the late husband, a night guard at the hilltop palace: tall, grey moustache, blue guard uniform and cap, torch.
Minor: a young female hospital social worker with red glasses; a young French couple; four elderly former guards in caps; the son from Germany with his family.

Hard rules: 16:9, no text or letters or numbers in the image, no watermarks, no logos, no shop signs, no flags, no license plates, no violence, no blood, no recognisable real people. Charts, listings, reviews, letters and phone screens are always blurred and unreadable. The distant hilltop palace is a generic red Moorish fortress without recognisable details. Recurring motifs: the beige hospital bedside phone, the old Spanish guitar on a whitewashed wall, the tiny yellow knitted baby jacket, the steep cobbled lane, the roof terrace view at sunset.
