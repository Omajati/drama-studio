PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 52-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«Tú no tienes familia, Tere: tu parte me la debes», dijo mi hermano. Yo reservé un vuelo". Story: at the reading of their mother's will, a 64-year-old childless widow, a retired haberdasher, is told by her younger brother that she owes him her share because he has a family and she does not. From inside her late mother's red velvet pincushion she pulls out his 1998 IOU for the money their mother lent him. He then produces a later will made in hospital that favours him. Under a sewing magnifier she finds that, in the same hospital night, her mother made him re-sign the debt in exchange. The inheritance is split fairly, and with her share she flies to Argentina to fulfil her mother's last wish: to bring the pincushion to her mother's estranged younger sister, who has an identical blue one. Winter in an old Castilian city, then summer by the Atlantic in Argentina at Christmas.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, cold clear Castilian winter light then bright Atlantic summer light, warm interiors, shallow depth of field, palette of red velvet, mustard, navy and sea blue, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.

Recurring characters (keep identical across shots):
TERE — a 64-year-old Southern European woman, short grey hair with a side fringe, small silver hoop earrings, a tape measure around her neck, mustard knitted cardigan over a navy blouse; outdoors a dark green wool coat.
JULIÁN — her 59-year-old brother, a mechanic, broad, thinning grey hair, paint-stained hands, checked-lined work jacket; as a young man in 1998 dark curly hair.
MAITE — the 57-year-old sister-in-law, dyed dark-red bob, fur-collared coat.
RODRIGO — the 33-year-old nephew, short dark beard, round glasses, blue puffer jacket.
ASUNCIÓN — the late mother, a seamstress: about 50 with dark hair in a bun and an apron; at 88 white-haired and frail.
PILAR — the 84-year-old aunt in Argentina, tiny, white hair in a low bun, blue flowered dress, knitted cardigan, arthritic fingers, in a wheelchair.
ERNESTO — her 62-year-old son, tall, white hair, sun-tanned, short-sleeved blue shirt.
Minor: a 60-year-old female lawyer with very short white hair and red glasses; a 65-year-old male notary.

Hard rules: 16:9, no text or letters or numbers in the image, no watermarks, no logos, no shop signs, no flags, no license plates, no violence, no blood, no recognisable real people. Documents, the IOU, letters, wills and phone screens are always blurred and unreadable. Locations look like an ordinary Southern European city and an ordinary South American seaside town, without national symbols or famous landmarks. Recurring motifs: the red velvet tomato-shaped pincushion with coloured-head pins, the identical blue one, the thin rolled sheet of squared notebook paper, the sewing magnifier lamp, the wicker sewing basket, the shoebox of letters tied with string.
