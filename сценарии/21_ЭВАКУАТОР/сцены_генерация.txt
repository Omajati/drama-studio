PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 48-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«La chatarra de su marido ya no afea la urbanización», dijo la presidenta. La grúa era mía". Story: on the second anniversary of her husband's death, a 70-year-old widow who ran the radio dispatch of the family tow company returns from the cemetery to find his restored 1978 sky-blue tow truck, nicknamed the Dove, gone from her own fenced plot. The estate's community president hands her the towing invoice, and it bears the logo of her own company. The request was signed by her son, the vice-president, who opened her gate with the key she gave him. At an extraordinary meeting she learns the president and her son plan to marry and move into her house, and to buy her vegetable plot for a padel court. She speaks up with the minutes in hand, the neighbours rise with her, the president pays and resigns, the son apologises publicly, she becomes president, opens her plot as a community garden, and finally lets her son drive the Dove again while the old radio crackles to life.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, warm bright Murcian light, dusty gold afternoons, shallow depth of field, palette of white walls, sky blue, lemon yellow and terracotta, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.
