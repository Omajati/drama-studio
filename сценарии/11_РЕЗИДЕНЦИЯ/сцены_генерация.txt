PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 60-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«Aquí estarás mejor, mamá»: en Nochebuena mi hija me regaló un folleto de residencia. Yo, un sobre". Story: on Christmas Eve a 68-year-old widow, a retired hardware-store bookkeeper, receives from her daughter a brochure for a care home, while her son-in-law says the flat will be "taken care of". She answers with a white envelope: in October she sold the bare ownership of her flat to the kind neighbours upstairs and kept the right to live there for life. The son-in-law, buried in debts from a failed bar, had secretly listed the flat and taken a deposit from a young couple with a newborn. Calmly, paper by paper, she makes him return the money, helps the young family, and gets her daughter back. Her late husband, a carpenter, carved one boxwood nativity figure every Christmas; the last one, kept hidden for three years, completes the nativity scene. Winter in a working-class neighbourhood of an inland Spanish city, cold wind, warm kitchen light.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, crisp winter light of an inland Spanish city, warm tungsten interiors, shallow depth of field, palette of cream walls, burgundy, warm wood and pale winter sky, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.
