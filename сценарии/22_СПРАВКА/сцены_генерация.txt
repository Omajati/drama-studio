PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 47-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«Mi madre ya no se acuerda de las cosas», le dijo mi hijo al banco. El banco me llamó a mí". Story: a 70-year-old widow, a retired postwoman, has just renewed her driving licence and was declared fit. Her son, a driving-school instructor, tells her bank she has memory problems so she cannot withdraw thirty thousand euros; the bank manager calls her, and she proves him wrong with the certificate. The money is for a used motorhome to follow the route around Spain her lorry-driver husband drew on a map before he died in a car crash twenty years ago. Her daughter-in-law reveals the son's nightmares; in a learner car at night the son confesses he was driving that night and his dying father swapped seats and invented a deer to save him. She sets conditions, the son teaches her to drive the motorhome and drives the first stage past the bend, and she completes the route, sending postcards from every stop and reading a hidden line on the map at the southern tip of Spain.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, dry golden light of the Spanish west, warm interiors, shallow depth of field, palette of ochre, holm-oak green, navy and postal yellow, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.
