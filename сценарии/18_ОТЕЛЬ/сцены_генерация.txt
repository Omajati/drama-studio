PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 52-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«Junto al ascensor estará más cómoda, señora», me dijo el director. Miró mi maleta, no mi apellido". Story: a 70-year-old widow, a retired schoolteacher, arrives by bus with an old suitcase for the fiftieth anniversary of a small seaside hotel her late husband co-founded. The young manager moves her to a noisy room by the lift. She lays on the counter the old brass key of room one and the shareholders' meeting notice: she owns forty per cent. Next morning her son, holding her proxy, is set to vote a capital increase that would shrink her share; she attends in person and blocks it with the founding statutes. The partner's son then produces a 2010 option signed by her husband; with old bank receipts, a chambermaid's memory and a former pupil now a lawyer, she proves the loan behind it was repaid and that half the shares were always hers. She sets her conditions, joins the board, and a year later sleeps in room one on her fiftieth wedding anniversary. November on the Mediterranean coast with a castle on a rock.
