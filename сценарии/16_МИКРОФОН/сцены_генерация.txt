PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 54-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«Mamá, ya te hemos reservado habitación», anunció mi hijo en mi jubilación. Luego hablé yo". Story: at her retirement party after forty-one years as a school secretary, a 66-year-old widow hears her son announce into the microphone that he has booked her a care-home room and will rent out her flat. She takes the microphone and reveals that half of his suburban house is hers by deed. She then discovers he forged her signature, paid a private doctor for a report declaring her cognitively impaired and asked a court to make him her legal guardian, with power to sell her half of the house. With her doctor, her sister, her daughter-in-law who turns ally, and a notarial deed she signed two years earlier naming her sister and excluding her son, she wins in court, sets her own conditions and finally walks the pilgrim road to Santiago with her best friend. Her motif is the habit of logging every document in green ledgers. Late autumn to spring in Madrid, then green pilgrim hills in May.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, crisp Madrid late-autumn light, warm interiors, shallow depth of field, palette of brick red, slate grey, plum and warm lamp gold, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.
