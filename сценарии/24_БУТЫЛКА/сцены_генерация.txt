PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 49-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«A mi hijo Lucas lo desheredo», leyó el notario. Yo tenía algo que su padre nunca supo". Story: a 69-year-old widow, a retired pastry-shop assistant in a wine region city, hears a notary read her former son-in-law's will disinheriting her 19-year-old grandson for "abandonment". That night she takes down a 1985 bottle her late winery-worker husband left her "for when it is needed": taped beneath it is proof of winery shares he secretly bought in her name for thirty years. She gives them to her grandson at once. The grandson plays voice messages and shows letters proving his father had reconciled with him and was about to change the will after finding the boy's letters hidden by his second wife. The widow's grandson recovers his inheritance by agreement, gives half to his young half-brothers, switches to winemaking, works his first harvest in his grandfather's winery, and the family opens the 1985 bottle on Christmas Eve.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, warm amber winter light, wine-cellar shadows, shallow depth of field, palette of deep wine red, oak brown, cream and gold, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.
