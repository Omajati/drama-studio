PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 42-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«Mi suegra no se entera de nada», reía mi nuera. La camarera me encerró para que lo oyera". Story: on Christmas Day a 67-year-old widow, a retired hairdresser in a rainy northern Spanish city, returns to a cider house for her forgotten phone. The waitress, whose mother she once helped, quietly locks the door and hides her behind a wooden screen; she hears her daughter-in-law laughing that she "never notices anything" and that a second loan in her name is approved. The owner shows her the CCTV footage of the daughter-in-law photographing her ID and card from her handbag. Next morning she blocks the second loan at the bank before the money moves. The daughter-in-law had been helping her sister pay loan sharks, who now held a copy of the widow's ID. The widow reports the loan sharks, not her daughter-in-law, sets her conditions, learns online banking at a library course, and a year later leaves her handbag open on the same chair: her daughter-in-law closes it.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, warm Christmas string-light glow against rainy northern blue-grey, shallow depth of field, palette of burgundy, wood brown, gold and rain grey, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.

Hard rules: 16:9, no text or letters or numbers in the image, no watermarks, no logos, no bank names, no shop signs, no flags, no license plates, no violence, no blood, no recognisable real people.
