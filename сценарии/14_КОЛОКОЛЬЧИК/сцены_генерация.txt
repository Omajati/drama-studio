PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 57-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«Con lo que usted tiene, señora, no nos compensa», dijo el director. No sabía de quién era el local". Story: a 71-year-old widow, a retired neighbourhood librarian, is told by a young bank manager that her account, with almost nothing left in it, is not worth his time. The bank branch sits in her late father's old corner grocery shop, which she has rented to the bank since 1998 on one condition: the brass shop bell above the door must stay. Her son, authorised on her account, had quietly drained her savings to save his failing building company, and the manager had pushed him to pledge her flat and then to get a power of attorney to sell the shop cheaply to the bank. Paper by paper, she refuses, files a complaint, renews the lease on her terms, has her son repay her month by month and finally tells her family the secret. Autumn to spring in an old Mediterranean city neighbourhood with a domed market.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, bright Mediterranean autumn light, warm interiors, shallow depth of field, palette of ochre, terracotta, navy and brass, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.
