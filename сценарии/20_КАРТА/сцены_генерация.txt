PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 47-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«¡Me han rechazado la tarjeta!», gritó mi nuera desde un spa. A mi marido lo enterramos el lunes". Story: four days after burying her fisherman husband, a 68-year-old widow, a retired fishmonger in a Galician port city, gets a call from her daughter-in-law screaming from a spa: her card has been declined. It was an extra card on the late husband's account, frozen when the widow brought the death certificate; half the money is hers. The bank shows the husband paid the daughter-in-law a monthly sum for two years. In a padlocked drawer on his boat the widow finds a tin of photos of a girl named Noelia, and under the boat's painted name another name: the daughter he had before meeting her. The daughter-in-law had been blackmailing him. The will leaves his half of the boat to his secret grandson. The widow sets conditions for the daughter-in-law, welcomes the daughter and grandson, and the boat is repainted with both women's names, one on each side.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, cold silver Atlantic light, warm interiors, shallow depth of field, palette of slate grey, white, navy blue and rust, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.
