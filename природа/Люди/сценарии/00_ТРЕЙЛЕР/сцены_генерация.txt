PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a one-minute English channel trailer for adults on the YouTube channel about prehistoric humans. Fast cuts: one image every one to two seconds, each image must be bold, readable at a glance, with one clear subject and strong light. Story: a child's fingerprint in clay 30,000 years ago; "they were not cavemen, they were us"; flour, bone needles, mammoth-bone houses, fire-making 400,000 years ago, long Ice Age nights; other humans (Neanderthals, Denisovans, the small people of Flores) and only one kind left; the Taung Child killed by an eagle almost three million years ago; first Europeans with no descendants and ancient DNA; the evidence (a tooth, a footprint, a hand stencil, new studies); fact, reconstruction, guess; ends with the same child and a line of people walking out of the dawn.

Visual style (used for every image): painterly realism, scientifically grounded reconstruction of prehistoric life, dramatic cinematic composition, high contrast, warm firelight against cold blue; documentary photograph look for fossils, museums and labs.

Recurring characters (keep identical across shots): THE CHILD and THE WOMAN exactly as in references R1 and R2 (the same people as in the channel's first video). People wear fitted sewn fur clothing, never loincloths. Neanderthals are stocky and upright, never ape-like. Scientists are generic figures seen from behind.

Hard rules: 16:9, no text or letters or numbers in the image, no watermarks, no logos, no blood, no wounds, no fighting, no hunting contact, no nudity, no cartoon or stick-figure style, no recognisable real people. Hands have five fingers. Screens and papers are blurred and unreadable.
