## 3. Описание
Thirty thousand years ago, on a hillside in what is now the Czech Republic, a group of Ice Age people woke up in huts held down by mammoth bones. What did they do with their day? And did they ever get an hour to themselves?

We follow one winter day, hour by hour, and every hour comes with a real clue: toe bones that prove they wore shoes, a grinding stone with 30,000-year-old flour, nets pressed into clay, the oldest ceramic workshop on Earth, a fingerprint left by a child, and a brand-new DNA study from October 2026 that links this valley to a woman at the far end of Europe. We tell you what is fact, what is reconstruction and what is a guess. The ending changes how you'll look at your coffee cup.
