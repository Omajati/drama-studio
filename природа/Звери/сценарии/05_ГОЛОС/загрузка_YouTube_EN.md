## 3. Описание
Scientists hid speakers next to waterholes in South Africa and played every sound a wild animal should fear: lions snarling, dogs barking, gunshots. Then they played something else. People. Just talking, calmly, like on the radio.

Giraffes ran. Hyenas ran. Leopards ran. Rhinos, animals that weigh as much as a pickup truck, ran. What do they actually hear in a human voice? Why did a mountain lion in California walk away from its own dinner because of a talk show? And if every animal is this afraid of us, why do some of them still hunt people?
