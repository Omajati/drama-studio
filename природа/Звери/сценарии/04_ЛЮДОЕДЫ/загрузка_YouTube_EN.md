## 3. Описание
In 1898, two lions walked into the camps of the men building a railway across East Africa. They came through thorn fences, past fires, into tents, for nine months. Hundreds of workers fled, construction stopped, and the Prime Minister had to explain it to Parliament.

That could be a story from the past. Except that in the largest mangrove forest on Earth, tigers still take people every year. Why there? Why these animals, when almost every big predator on the planet avoids us? And how many people did the Lions of Tsavo really eat? The famous number turned out to be wrong, and the proof was hiding in their broken teeth.
