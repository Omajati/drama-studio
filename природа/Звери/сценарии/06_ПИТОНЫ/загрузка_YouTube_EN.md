## 3. Описание
Scientists drove the roads of Everglades National Park at night for years, counting every animal in their headlights. When they compared the counts with the nineteen-nineties, the raccoons were almost gone. The opossums were almost gone. The rabbits and foxes weren't seen at all. No disease, no poison, no hunters.

The killer arrived in Florida as a pet. The biggest are now longer than a car, and the people searching for them almost never see one, even when they know it's right there. How do you prove what happened to animals nobody saw die? Why does Florida use snakes to hunt snakes? And how close have they already come to the people living next door?
