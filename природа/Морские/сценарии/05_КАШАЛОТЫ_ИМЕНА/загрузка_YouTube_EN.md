## 3. Описание
Off the Caribbean island of Dominica, the same sperm whale families have been followed and recorded since 2005. When they come up from a two-kilometer dive, they gather close and trade short rhythms of clicks. One of those rhythms, five clicks long, has never been heard anywhere outside the Caribbean, and it seems to say one thing: which clan you belong to.

Is it a family name? Do individual whales have names of their own? What did Project CETI find when it fed thousands of codas into a machine, and what did a sperm whale family sound like the day a calf was born? Here's what scientists actually know, and what is still a guess.
