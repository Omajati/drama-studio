## 3. Описание
In July 2016, a gold miner thawing frozen mud in Canada's Yukon watched a small, furry shape slide out of the wall. It was a wolf pup, curled up as if asleep, with her fur, ears and paws still in place. She had been lying there for about 57,000 years.

How does a puppy stay whole that long, and why was she alone in the ground? We follow the chemistry in her hair to a dinner nobody expected from an Ice Age wolf, meet two Siberian lion cubs found side by side who never met, a baby woolly rhino, a "puppy" called Dogor, and two famous Ice Age "puppies" that a 2025 study unmasked. And at the end, Zhùr turns out to be telling us about someone else entirely.
