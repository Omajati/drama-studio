PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 17-minute English science documentary for adults on the YouTube channel about dinosaurs and the prehistoric world, titled "This Wolf Pup Slept for 57,000 Years. Then a Gold Miner Woke Her". Story: in July 2016 a gold miner thawing frozen mud with a water cannon at Last Chance Creek near Dawson City (Yukon, Canada) uncovered Zhùr, a mummified gray wolf pup about seven weeks old who lived about 57,000 years ago, the most complete Ice Age wolf mummy ever found (Meachen, Zazula et al., Current Biology, 2020). Her hair and teeth show a diet based on river food, probably salmon; she most likely died instantly when her den collapsed; she was found alone. Other permafrost babies: the baby mammoth Nun cho ga (Yukon, 2022), the cave lion cubs Boris and Sparta (Yakutia, born about 15,000 years apart), the baby woolly rhino Sasha, the puppy Dogor (a wolf), and the Tumat "puppies" (a 2025 study: wolf sisters whose last meal was woolly rhinoceros). Twist: the river signal in a seven-week pup comes largely through her mother's milk, so Zhùr is a record of a fishing mother wolf never found. Opens and ends at the frozen mine wall and the water cannon; teaser for Nanotyrannus.

Visual style (used for every image): painterly realism paleoart for the Ice Age world and living animals; documentary photograph look for the mine, labs, museums and mummies; overcast northern documentary look with mist and spray for the Klondike and Siberian digs; clean flat scientific illustration for size comparisons, maps, timelines and diagrams.

Recurring characters (keep identical across shots):
ZHÙR (MUMMY) — a small mummified gray wolf pup about seven weeks old, curled on her side like a sleeping puppy: two small upright ears, short snout, front paws tucked under the chin, thin tail along the body, dark brown and grey fur still on, skin darkened and slightly shrunken, eyelids closed and sunken. Always clearly a puppy, never a rat, never a lump of mud.
ZHÙR (ALIVE) — the same pup alive in the Ice Age: a fluffy grey-brown wolf pup with big paws, round belly, dark eyes, small ears.
MOTHER WOLF — an Ice Age gray wolf: robust, heavy head with strong jaws, thick grey-brown coat, long legs, often wet from the river.
KLONDIKE MINE — a placer gold mine in a creek valley: tall cliffs of black-grey frozen muck, a large water cannon on a stand, mud, gravel, excavators, spruce hills, no signs or logos.
SIBERIAN RIVERBANK — a thawing permafrost bank on a remote river in Yakutia: larch trees, grey water, exposed frozen soil.
Miners, hunters, scientists and elders are generic figures seen from behind or with faces out of frame.

Hard rules: 16:9, no text or letters or numbers in the image, no watermarks, no logos, no company names, no signs, no flags, no blood, no wounds, no fighting, no hunting contact, no cartoon style, no recognisable real people. Screens, papers, journals and diagrams are always blurred and unreadable. Dinosaurs follow modern science: feathered tyrannosaurs where appropriate, lips covering the teeth. Woolly mammoths have small ears. Mummies are shown with respect, never as gore.
