## 3. Описание
In April 2025, a company in Texas announced that the dire wolf, gone since the end of the Ice Age, was alive again. Three pale pups. A magazine cover. Within days, the scientists who study wild dogs for a living said the animal had never come back at all.

So what is actually living behind that fence? We follow a 13,000-year-old tooth from an Ohio gravel pit, two research teams that drew the dire wolf's family tree in very different ways, the twenty gene edits at the centre of the fight, and what the three animals are like now, at two years old.
