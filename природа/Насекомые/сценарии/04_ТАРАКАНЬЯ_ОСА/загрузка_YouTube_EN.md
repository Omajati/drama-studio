## 3. Описание
You've chased a cockroach across a kitchen floor. You know how fast it runs. So why would one walk calmly into its own grave, led by an insect half its size?

The emerald cockroach wasp stings a cockroach twice: once in the body, once in the brain, with a stinger that can feel its way to the right spot. The cockroach isn't dead and isn't paralyzed. It can walk, swim and turn itself over. It just stops wanting to. What the venom switches off, why the cockroach stays fresh for days, how some cockroaches karate-kick their way out, and what all this taught scientists about motivation, including yours.
