PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 13-minute English YouTube nature documentary for adults, channel "Insects" of the Animals network, titled "Every Venomous Spider That Could Be in Your House, Ranked". Story: a ladder of eight spiders that really live in houses in the United States, Europe and Australia, ranked by what a bite can do to a healthy adult, from harmless to a true emergency: the cellar spider (daddy longlegs) on the bathroom ceiling, the common house spider, the giant house spider and its cousin the hobo spider, the yellow sac spider, the noble false widow, the brown recluse (a Kansas farmhouse with two thousand recluses and no bites), the black widow and the Australian redback, and at the top the male Sydney funnel-web, whose antivenom is made from spiders that ordinary people catch in jars. Second line: that red bump people blame on a spider is usually a skin infection. Ordinary homes at night and dawn, bathrooms, bedrooms, garages, gardens, laboratories in daylight. Calm, respectful, never horror.

Visual style (used for most images): photorealistic macro nature photography, shallow depth of field, natural soft light, crisp detail of eight eyes in groups and fine leg hairs on spiders (no compound eyes, no wings on spiders). Diagrams, maps and comparisons: vintage entomology plate illustration in ink and watercolor on cream paper.

Recurring characters (keep identical across shots; each has a reference image R1–R8):
CELLAR SPIDER (R1) — tiny pale beige elongated body smaller than a grain of rice, eight extremely long hair-thin legs.
HOUSE SPIDER (R2) — small round mottled brown-grey abdomen the size of a pea, eight banded legs.
GIANT HOUSE SPIDER (R3) — long-legged brown spider, hairy banded legs, pale chevron pattern, leg span as wide as a child's palm.
SAC SPIDER (R4) — pale straw-yellow, almost translucent, smaller than a fingernail, dark jaws and leg tips.
FALSE WIDOW (R5) — glossy round dark-brown abdomen with cream markings, reddish-brown legs.
RECLUSE (R6) — uniform tan, long thin legs, dark violin mark on the front of the body, six eyes in three pairs.
WIDOW (R7) — glossy jet-black round abdomen; black widow with a red hourglass underneath, redback with a red stripe on the back.
FUNNEL-WEB (R8) — male Sydney funnel-web, glossy blue-black, large downward fangs, finger-like spinnerets, big enough to cover a matchbox.

Hard rules: 16:9, no text or letters or numbers in the image, no watermarks, no logos, no signs, no labels on diagrams or maps, no recognisable real people (people only as hands, arms, feet or silhouettes from behind). Anatomy in every spider shot: exactly eight legs and no antennae; insects six legs and two antennae. No blood, no open wounds, no crushed animals, no spider fangs in a human face. Phobia rule: no large spider close-up in scenes S001–S011 (the first minute) — only rooms, corners, threads, shadows, distant tiny shapes.
