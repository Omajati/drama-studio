PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 75-second Russian-language channel trailer (welcome video) for the YouTube channel «Сан Саныч из Союза» (folder «Истории RU»), titled "Здравствуй, я Сан Саныч из Союза. Как жили в СССР на самом деле — честно и с цифрами". Fast, dynamic cuts (about 3 seconds per image): four surprising facts about Soviet life (being late to work as a court matter in 1940, a car costing 45 average salaries, collective farmers without passports, the thirteenth salary once a year), then the narrator SAN SANYCH introduces the channel: money, festive tables, flats awaited for years, New Year, work, childhood — every price with a source, then and now. He is a character (the channel's narrator image), never shown with a visible face.

Visual style (used for every image): photorealistic cinematic documentary stills; 1940 in muted colours; 1970s in warm natural colours with lamp light; cool modern documentary photos for the present day. Interiors, clothes, cars and objects strictly of their decade.

Recurring characters (keep identical across shots):
SAN SANYCH — an elderly Soviet man over seventy: flat eight-panel cap, horn-rimmed glasses, thick grey moustache, grey hair at the neck, knitted cardigan over a checked shirt, big working hands. ALWAYS shown from behind, as a silhouette against light, or only his hands — his face is never visible.
VIEWER — a person in their 30s, casual clothes, modern apartment.

Hard rules: 16:9, no text or letters or numbers in the image, no watermarks, no logos, no shop signs, no flags, no political symbols or portraits of leaders, no slogans, no license plates, no violence, no blood. Documents, labour books, passports, newspapers, notebooks, books and phone screens are always blurred and unreadable. SAN SANYCH's face never appears.
