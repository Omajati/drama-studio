PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 30-minute Russian-language YouTube documentary for adults, channel «Истории RU», titled "Он не мог ЗАБЫТЬ ничего. Учёный изучал его 30 лет и понял, зачем ты забываешь". Story: in 1920s Moscow a newspaper reporter, Solomon Shereshevsky, never takes notes because he remembers everything. The young psychologist Alexander Luria studies him for thirty years: unlimited memory, colours and tastes for every sound, memorising by walking down a street in his mind. The gift ruins his life: he cannot understand poems or abstract ideas, cannot recognise faces, cannot forget, even tries to burn papers with numbers and still sees them on the ashes. He learns to forget only by not looking. Mirror story: Luria's wounded patient Lev Zasetsky who lost his memory and rebuilt it in a diary of thousands of pages. Today: people with superior autobiographical memory, sleep that cleans the brain, and smartphones as memory without a bottom.

Visual style (used for every image): photorealistic cinematic documentary stills of 1920s–1950s Soviet Moscow (muted colours, warm lamps, film grain); surreal glowing dream images for the inner world of memory and synaesthesia; cool modern documentary photos for the present day.

Recurring characters (keep identical across shots):
SHERESHEVSKY — in the 1920s a thin 40-year-old man, dark wavy hair combed back, high forehead, attentive dark calm eyes, clean-shaven, worn grey three-piece suit, white shirt with a narrow dark tie; in the 1950s a 68-year-old man with thinning grey hair, the same eyes, dark stage suit with a bow tie.
LURIA — in the 1920s–1930s a 28-year-old man with dark wavy hair, small round wire glasses, lively smile, white lab coat over a shirt and tie or a grey suit; in the 1950s–1960s a bald 60-year-old man with round glasses and a dark suit.
VYGOTSKY — a thin pale 32-year-old man with dark hair swept back, high forehead, intense eyes, dark suit.
ZASETSKY — in 1943 a 23-year-old junior officer in a plain Soviet army tunic without insignia, head bandaged; later a 45-year-old man with short greying hair and a scar on the left side of the head, cardigan, writing at a desk.
EDITOR — a stout 50-year-old man with a moustache, sleeves rolled up, waistcoat, pencil behind his ear.
MODERN WOMAN — a woman in her 40s seen from behind or in profile, dark hair, cardigan, in a sunny California living room (not a real person).
VIEWER — a person in their 30s, casual clothes, seen in profile or from behind, holding a smartphone.

Hard rules: 16:9, no text or letters or numbers in the image, no watermarks, no logos, no shop signs, no flags, no political symbols, no license plates, no violence, no blood, no recognisable real people (historical figures are described by period appearance only). Documents, newspapers, notebooks, chalkboards, tables of digits and phone screens are always blurred and unreadable. Recurring motifs: the empty notebook, a chalkboard with a blurred grid, burning paper, the grey suit, the street of memory with objects placed along it.
