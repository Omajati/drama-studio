PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 30-minute Russian-language YouTube documentary for adults, channel «Истории RU», titled "Он понял, как в тебе ЗАПИСАНА жизнь, за 25 лет до разгадки ДНК. Его объявили ЛЖЕУЧЁНЫМ". Story: Soviet biologist Nikolai Koltsov. In December 1927, at a congress in Leningrad, he proposes that heredity is written in one giant molecule — a long thread with genes in a row — which copies itself by building a mirror copy on itself, link by link (the template principle, a quarter of a century before the double helix of 1953). His life: in 1920 he is sentenced to death in a show trial and released the same night; he builds his own Institute of Experimental Biology in a Moscow manor; he refuses to renounce genetics when Lysenko's campaign begins; in 1939 a newspaper letter and an Academy commission remove him as director; in December 1940 he dies in a Leningrad hotel, and his wife dies days later. His idea travels through his student Timofeev-Ressovsky in Berlin to Schrödinger's book and to Watson and Crick. Today: your cells copying the text right now, genetic tests, newborn screening, gene editing — and his honest mistakes (he thought the molecule was protein; he supported eugenics in the 1920s).

Visual style (used for every image): photorealistic cinematic documentary stills of 1900s–1940s Moscow and Leningrad (muted colours, warm lamps, film grain); Western Europe and America 1930s–1950s in the same muted style; surreal glowing images of threads, cells and molecules on a dark background for the inner world of heredity; cool modern documentary photos for the present day.

Recurring characters (keep identical across shots):

Hard rules: 16:9, no text or letters or numbers in the image, no watermarks, no logos, no shop signs, no flags, no political symbols, no license plates, no violence, no blood, no recognisable real people (historical figures are described by period appearance only).
