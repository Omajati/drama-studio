PROJECT CONTEXT:
Project: a 60-minute YouTube family drama for adults, channel La Matriarca Silenciosa, titled "«Tú sigue con tus croquetas», se rió mi cuñado: a mi hermana, la casa; a mí, la lata de recetas". Story: a 66-year-old widow, a retired school canteen cook, inherits only her late mother's blue tin of handwritten recipe cards, while her younger sister gets the white house with green shutters above the sea in an old fishermen's quarter. One yellow card, "rice pudding for Lola", hides the mother's real handwritten will; pencil notes in the margins reveal years of control by the brother-in-law. The old neighbour across the street saw the will being written — guided by the sister herself. The sisters make rice pudding in their mother's kitchen and later repaint the shutters together. Late autumn to spring, grey sea, rain, warm kitchen light.

Visual style (used for every image): cinematic photorealistic film still, soft overcast northern-coast light, shallow depth of field, palette of sea grey, white walls, faded green shutters and warm kitchen lamps, gentle film grain, intimate family drama.

Recurring characters (keep identical across shots):
LOLA — a sturdy 66-year-old Southern European woman, short curly grey hair, round tortoiseshell glasses, rosy cheeks, strong cook's hands, dark green wool cardigan over a white blouse.
SISTER — a slim 61-year-old woman, chin-length salon-blonde straight hair, pearl earrings, camel coat; at home a grey tracksuit, no make-up, hair in a claw clip.
BROTHER-IN-LAW — a tall 64-year-old man, grey hair combed back, reading glasses low on his nose, navy blazer, light blue shirt, gold signet ring; later an old grey jumper, unshaven.
DAUGHTER — a 40-year-old woman, dark hair in a ponytail, black-framed glasses, navy office blazer.
NEIGHBOUR — a tiny 88-year-old woman, white hair in a hairnet, very thick glasses, black dress and black knitted shawl, wooden cane, knotted knuckles.
MOTHER — in the 1970s a strong woman of about 38 with a white apron and dark hair in a bun; at 92 a small frail woman with white hair and a beige cardigan, alert eyes.

Hard rules: 16:9, no text or letters or numbers in the image, no watermarks, no logos, no shop signs, no flags, no license plates, no violence, no blood, no recognisable real people. Phone screens, documents, cards and brochures are always blurred and unreadable. Locations look like an ordinary Southern European city without national symbols; no national dishes in close-up. Recurring motifs: the round blue biscuit tin full of yellowed recipe cards, the yellow card in an old envelope, the white bowl of rice pudding with a cinnamon cross, the green shutters, the window over the sea.
